# Educational Goals and Reflection

My five-year goals focus on building a career in UX design, developing my creative skills, becoming financially independent, and creating a fulfilling personal life. These goals are focused on what I want to accomplish after university, but they also connect to the idea that learning and personal development are ongoing processes.

In *Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection, Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process*, Susan F. Belgrad explains that portfolios can show a student's effort, progress, and achievement over time. She also discusses how reflection and self-assessment can help students understand their progress, set their own goals, and continue developing new goals. This relates to my five-year plan because my goals are not only final achievements. They also give me something to work toward and a way to reflect on how I am progressing. My professional and creative goals especially depend on continuously improving my skills and learning from the work I produce. However, my financial and personal goals extend beyond traditional academic learning and focus more on the kind of life I want to build after graduating.

## Reference

Belgrad, S. F. (2013). Portfolios and e-portfolios: Student reflection, self-assessment, and goal setting in the learning process. In J. H. McMillan (Ed.), *SAGE handbook of research on classroom assessment*. SAGE Publications. https://doi.org/10.4135/9781452218649.n19
